[Purpose] This is a research request to raise the quality of a Japanese nursing-assessment support website as a whole. It is NOT about any one patient. Below are (A) common diseases and symptoms the site holds little or no knowledge about, and (B) structural topics where general judgment tables are missing. Answer B first: it has the largest effect. Collect nursing and medical knowledge normally: disease-specific, symptom-specific, drug-specific and test-specific knowledge are all wanted and will be stored as knowledge rows; write it so that it holds for every patient with that disease, symptom or drug.
[Generalization] For every topic state the general condition it rests on (disease group, symptom class, lab key, phase, age group, sex, drug class), say whether it also applies to related groups, and mark patient-specific or one-case advice as such. Disease-specific content is welcome (the scope line says who it applies to); prefer table rows over narrative, but never drop specific knowledge for being specific.
[Sources] Prefer Japanese textbooks (title, edition, page) and official guidelines (name, year, page). Write 不明 where unknown; never fill gaps with guesses.
[New categories] If a piece of information does not fit any of the 14 needs or the existing concept tags without distortion, do not force it. Say where it belongs and whether a NEW concept/category is warranted: give the general condition (disease group, phase, subject, age group), at least 3 distinct cases it would cover, and why an existing destination is insufficient.

## B. 構造的な不足（表の形で集めてください）
1. 術式・処置ごとの術後経過の時期別観察表（胃切除・大腸切除・肝胆膵・開胸・開腹・人工関節・帝王切開・心臓外科）：合併症（縫合不全・出血・イレウス・肺合併症・静脈血栓・ダンピング・栄養障害など）の好発時期、観察項目、看護、退院後の生活（食事・仕事・外食・旅行）への影響。診断を決めない書き方で
